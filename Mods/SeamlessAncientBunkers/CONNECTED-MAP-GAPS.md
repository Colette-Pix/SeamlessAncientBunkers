# Connected maps: remaining implementation audit

Reviewed 2026-09-27 against the live mod source, installed RimWorld 1.6.4871 decompilation, existing test reports, and the two active implementation chats. This is a source audit and implementation backlog, not a claim that every scenario below has failed a gameplay test. No gameplay code, assembly, configuration, or colony save was changed by this audit.

**Conclusion:** the current mod provides substantial shared-colony behavior, but several systems still select workers, recipients, supplies, or group members from only one physical map. Making all ordinary colony operations work across levels requires complete actions that can fetch, carry, travel, and finish, in addition to shared menus and resource totals.

**Excluded from new work**

Already implemented: core work travel; ordinary stockpile hauling; construction/production ingredient staging; shared resource displays and building-material selection; management rosters and assignment menus; ordinary food, rest, medical-rest and building-recreation routing; drugs, outfits, dining and Dubs thirst; power and Dubs plumbing; basic animal needs/following; Misc. Robots work/charging; selected caravan and ritual participants; hostile assault pursuit; basic gravship safeguards; cross-level manual orders, selection and order feedback.

Currently being implemented or tested in **Fix cross-map hunting and animal AI**: hunting, attack-trained animal pursuit, VPE summoned skeleton combat, completed milking and shearing. Currently being implemented or tested in **Check cross-map burial and surgery**: corpse-to-grave transport, surgery supply delivery, surgery completion, and patients recognizing reachable remote doctors. These remain owned by those efforts.

Presence in this baseline does not mean every combination has been tested.

**Adjacent to current work: verify before opening duplicate tasks**

- **Medicine for ordinary tending:** current medical delivery recognizes Doctor work through DoBill and Bill_Medical. Ordinary tending uses FindBestMedicine, which checks the patient's map and the doctor's/local animal inventories. Verify fetching remote medicine for wounds, infection, childbirth and animal tending as part of the current medical effort. Never delay lifesaving no-medicine treatment merely to fetch a better drug. See [LinkedLogistics.cs](<C:/Program Files (x86)/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/Mods/SeamlessAncientBunkers/Source/LinkedLogistics.cs:28>) and [HealthAIUtility.FindBestMedicine](<C:/Program Files (x86)/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/BunkerBuildInspection/VanillaAudit/Assembly-CSharp.decompiled.cs:795874>).
- **Owned beds and better facilities:** ordinary remote sleep planning returns early when vanilla finds any local bed. Test a remote owned double bed against an unowned local bed, spouses split between floors, and designated medical care. Existing floor-sleep and remote-bed fixes should be extended only if these tests reveal a mismatch.
- **Material accessibility and competing requests:** staging counts broad map-local stocks by definition. A stockpile behind an inaccessible door, outside the worker's allowed area, outside a bill's ingredient radius, or reserved for another consumer can suppress a needed delivery. Test multiple benches/build sites competing for one small stack, substitution rules, forbidden changes and container-held stock.
- **Shared warnings versus reachable services:** roster sharing can remove a “no warden/mechanitor/bed” warning while the corresponding action is unsupported or its route is forbidden/unsafe. Availability checks should distinguish colony membership from actual service availability; avoid reopening already-fixed roster menus.
- **Research facilities:** remote researchers already travel to benches. Verify multianalyzers, linked facilities and other bench prerequisites when the facility is on a different floor. Adjacent-building effects need explicit connection semantics; a shared roster cannot satisfy them.

**Systems requiring a rule decision or focused regression, not a blanket merge**

- **Heat, smoke/gas, fire and atmosphere:** decide whether open entrances exchange these and whether sealing blocks exchange. Model exchange at an opening if desired. Separate floors should retain local rooms, roofs, outdoor weather exposure, beauty, cover and physical coordinates. Power/plumbing support does not imply shared room simulation.
- **Combat geometry and abilities:** define portal traversal versus line of sight, ranged attacks, explosions, auras and psycast range. Ordinary walls/floors should still obstruct effects. Route distance can support navigation without allowing attacks through arbitrary map coordinates.
- **Colony accounting and incidents:** native WealthWatcher already adds child pocket-map wealth to the source map, and DefaultThreatPointsNow redirects a pocket-map target to its source. Do not sum those values again. Test population contribution, adaptation, threat visibility, quest presence checks and event ownership while all colonists are downstairs, after disconnection and after moving a hatch. See [WealthWatcher pocket-map aggregation](<C:/Program Files (x86)/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/BunkerBuildInspection/VanillaAudit/Assembly-CSharp.decompiled.cs:497528>) and [StorytellerUtility.DefaultThreatPointsNow](<C:/Program Files (x86)/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/BunkerBuildInspection/VanillaAudit/Assembly-CSharp.decompiled.cs:459806>).
- **Map lifecycle:** existing gravship safeguards are implemented. Extend regression coverage to prisoners/non-player guests, contained pawns, abandoned maps, destroyed/sealed entrances, relocated portals, and a player leaving the last reachable map while cargo or passengers are in transit. The gravship occupant guard currently checks spawned player-faction pawns; other occupants need a separate review.
- **Mod integrations:** independent third-party needs, loadouts, storage containers, gas/pipe networks and custom group AI need specific adapters or documented limits. Existing Misc. Robots, Quarry, Processor, Dubs and VPE support does not imply universal compatibility.

**Implementation order**

1. Living-pawn carrying and safe emergency routing.
2. Wardening, childcare, slave and general mech eligibility, using the carrying foundation.
3. Finish ordinary work coverage and generalized supply fetching; include corpse processing.
4. Correct filtered bill counts, remote bill destinations, trading and departure cargo.
5. Add special needs, social/group behavior, pen operations and DLC workflows.
6. Complete queueing, scheduling fairness, alternative-route recovery and lifecycle/accounting regressions. Address any fairness defect exposed by earlier steps immediately.

Every new workflow should pass both directions, sibling bunkers, multiple hops, save/reload while walking and carrying, disconnect/reconnect, a forbidden endpoint, changed allowed areas, a moved/destroyed target, competing workers, and a relevant danger case. Verify completed outcomes and item/pawn conservation, not just whether a travel job was created. Continue to use disposable engine fixtures.

The coherent implementation foundation is a shared connected-colony query layer plus action-specific travel, fetching and carrying. Keep physical map lists and coordinates local; extend systems at their actual decision and execution points.
