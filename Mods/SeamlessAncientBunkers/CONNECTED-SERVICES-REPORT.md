## Execution and safety details

- `ConsumerSupply` records the consumer and work giver in the existing saved intent. A worker may travel empty to a source, physically collect stock, travel back, stage it within the consumer's permitted area/radius, and let the native work giver consume it. Fuel, hopper thresholds/priority, bill filters/mixing/skills, medicine policy, childcare and prisoner feeding restrictions remain native or explicitly checked.
- Availability uses physically reachable, reservable, permitted stock instead of broad map totals. Bill ingredient radius is respected. Active deliveries and local carried supply are considered to avoid redundant requests. Source/consumer/route conditions are checked again at each leg.
- `ServicesInventory` exposes spawned items, actual `IHaulSource` contents, and searchable gene-bank packs. It does not search arbitrary pawn inventories or sealed containers. Explicit held-item extraction reserves and reaches the physical holder before moving the same item into the carrier.
- Ground trading uses the native single-confirmation transaction. Native validation finishes before sale side effects. Native stack splitting/payment handles remote goods and silver; sold animals run native pre-trade cleanup on their original floor, then enter the trader's native faction/lord on its floor. Sold whole-stack delivery claims are released only during a validated sale so native inventory merging remains possible. Obsolete trade-staging requests from earlier development saves are removed on load while unrelated explicit deliveries are preserved.
- Ordinary hauling can start beside the item or travel empty to pick it up on another floor. After pickup, storage is revalidated and the pawn physically carries/drops the item through native jobs. Queries themselves do not move pawns, reserve targets or create travel intents. Existing work priorities, forbidden/allowed-area rules, stockpile filters/priorities and local equal-priority preference remain authoritative. Source clutter does not need a Home designation or a local stockpile.
- Orbital trading requires a powered beacon on the floor containing the goods. It does not project coverage through a hatch. Purchases continue to arrive on the ship's native map. Remote sellable pawns use their local beacon coverage.
- `ServiceDeliveries` saves outstanding explicit/bill item requests, carried item identities, remaining source references and compatibility receipts. A split pickup follows the selected portion across interruptions instead of reselecting unrelated stock. Requested stacks temporarily keep distinct identity; placement selects a free cell rather than attempting a forbidden merge. Partial trips decrement only physically delivered quantities. Removal preparation clears requests/completion IDs/receipts without interrupting unrelated native jobs. Direct trading creates no requests or receipt IDs.
- Doctor/warden availability warnings use reachable workers; management roster sharing remains separate. Recipe facilities, research prerequisites, classroom geometry, gene-bank access by an assembler, room temperature, room boundaries and other adjacency effects remain local.
- A native gene-bank helper now rejects unspawned packs before querying their nonexistent `Map`. Packs already stored in banks require an explicit request or target assignment to be extracted; ordinary storage hauling does not empty banks.

## Remaining limits and untested combinations

This is representative engine acceptance, not exhaustive proof of every scanner and combination in the audit. In particular:

- Full prisoner interaction/feeding/cell-transfer cases, baby-to-mother and safe-temperature carrying, cremation, every maintenance/DLC consumer, meditation and Dubs toilet/bathing are not individually completed engine fixtures here. They are implemented through the audited native queries/transport interfaces.
- Every new service has not been run in both directions, sibling/multiple-hop configurations, and every disconnect, danger, forbidden, moved-target, competing-worker and save/reload permutation. Component carrying and an interrupted partial delivery request have actual fresh-process reload tests; other request types do not all have their own reload fixture. Ordinary hauling does have native completion in both directions and with either source/worker placement.
- Painting work can travel to a map with native usable dye; a dedicated missing-dye delivery request for floor-paint designations is not implemented. Generic storage hauling can supply such a map. Work-scanner inclusion does not imply a universal ingredient adapter for all possible mod work givers.
- Research multianalyzers and other adjacency-based building effects are not shared between floors. Gene assembly still requires native local gene-bank availability; explicit pack movement is physical. No heat/gas or ranged-combat rules were changed.
- Supported third-party adapters remain explicit (Dubs, Processor, Quarry and pre-existing robot integrations). Arbitrary custom storage, needs and group frameworks are outside the compatibility claim.

Transport, caravan/pod/shuttle cargo, passenger lifecycle, graph alternatives, slave/mech/group behavior, and final release validation are recorded separately by the transport owner.
